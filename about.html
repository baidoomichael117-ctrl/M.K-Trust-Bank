Since 2010

Our history.

Sixteen years of steady, deliberate growth — one branch, one relationship at a time.

2010

A single room in Accra

M.K Trust Bank opens its first branch on East Legon with four staff and a promise to treat every depositor's cedi like our own.

2014

First loan book for traders

We launch our first working-capital loans for market traders in Makola, funding over 500 small businesses in the first year alone.

2016

Expanding beyond Accra

New branches open in Kumasi, Takoradi and Akropong as customer numbers cross 10,000, marking our first steps outside Greater Accra.

2020

Mobile banking goes live

We launch mobile and agency banking so customers can save, send and pay bills without visiting a branch.

2026

8 branches and counting

Today we serve more than 24,000 customers from 8 branches, still guided by the same founding promise.

Leadership

A word from our CEO.

Chief Executive Officer & Founder

Michael Kwabena Baidoo

Michael Kwabena Baidoo founded Baidoo Trust Bank in 2010 after a decade working in retail banking across Accra, where he saw how many hardworking families and traders were underserved by larger institutions.

Under his leadership, the bank has grown from a single branch to twelve, while holding onto the same principle he started with: banking should be simple, honest, and close to home. He is a strong believer in financial literacy and personally visits branches each quarter to meet customers and staff.

Outside the bank, Michael mentors young entrepreneurs and sits on the board of two community development initiatives in Greater Accra.

"Every account we open is a promise. We intend to keep it." — Michael Kwabena Baidoo
